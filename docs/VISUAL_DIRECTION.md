# Visual direction

**Idea:** a cathedral of violet light, with one enormous, quiet figure in it.

- **Colour:** deep violet and indigo own the frame. Each nebula brings a second colour (magenta,
  azure, teal, ember). Gold is rare and means attention: his eyes, the astrolabe, the sun, the
  interface accent.
- **Never black:** shadow sides are filled with violet, and the grade lifts blacks toward indigo.
- **Light:** one sun, raking from the side, so worlds show their form. The default composition puts
  the camera at about 90 degrees to the sun for that reason.
- **Space:** worlds sit well apart, so each one gets its own patch of sky. The interface follows
  the same rule, with generous margins and room between lines.
- **Depth:** baked sky, real stars, galaxies, fly-through fog, near dust.
- **The Watcher:** a colossus nearly 3,000 units tall, roughly seven Jupiters high. A dark column
  of cloak, a pale blank mask in the void of a hood, slits of light, a turning gold astrolabe. He
  stands far back and to one side, with Earth in front of him for scale, and the haze between him
  and the camera is what sells his size.
- **Interface:** type set directly on space, with no panels behind it. Serif for names, sans for
  small text. The only filled shapes are the current mode and the one primary action.
- **Restraint:** bloom only fires above HDR 1.3, and chromatic aberration is barely there until you
  enter his eyes. If an effect is noticeable, it is too strong.
